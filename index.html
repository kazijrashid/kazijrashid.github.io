Hi! I am a Ph.D. student and Graduate Assistant in the Department of Geography at Florida State University, advised by Dr. Christopher K. Uejio. Previously, I earned my M.S. and B.S. in Geography and Environmental Studies from the University of Chittagong.

My research centers on Geospatial AI (GeoAI) and Urban Environmental Modeling, coupling Earth observation data, deep learning surrogates, and microclimate physics to analyze thermal stress, urban heat islands, and urban microclimates.

Research Focus

My current and prospective research investigates:

  • Geospatial Foundation Models (GeoFMs) & ViTs: Adapting vision transformers and masked autoencoders for spatial raster analysis and turbulent kinetic energy estimation.
  • Physics-Informed Deep Learning & CFD Surrogates: Replacing intensive CFD microclimate simulations with neural operators for street-canyon aerodynamics and thermal comfort (UTCI/PET).
  • Satellite Thermal Downscaling & Microclimate: Synergizing Sentinel-2, Landsat-9, and ECOSTRESS thermal infrared data with surface aerodynamic roughness parameters.
  • Urban Surface Dynamics & Environmental Equity: Long-term land cover transformations, urban heat island (UHI) feedbacks, and nonlinear environmental drivers via GeoXAI.
